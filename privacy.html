1 Short Summary

MYA PDF is a productivity tool that works exclusively on the Egyptian e-invoicing portals (invoicing.eta.gov.eg and profile.eta.gov.eg). It lets you download, submit and sign e-invoices and e-receipts, export PDF/Excel files, and build VAT and Form 41 reports and lightweight ERP records. It is an independent personal product, not affiliated with or endorsed by any government entity.

Your documents go only where you send them. Invoices and receipts are processed on your machine. They are sent to the Egyptian Tax Authority only when you ask to submit or download them, and to our cloud only when you use the multi-device sync and ERP features (see sections 2 and 3).
License data is always sent. To verify a paid subscription and enforce the device limit, the extension sends the email used to log into the tax portal, a random device ID, browser information and the extension version to the developer's server. The server may also record your IP address.

2 Data We Collect and Why

2.1 Email address

MYA PDF is a paid-license product. The extension reads the email of the signed-in user on the tax portal and sends it to the developer's server (Supabase) to verify an active subscription and to enforce the device limit.

2.2 IP address

The IP address may be recorded server-side with license-check requests. It is used exclusively to prevent abuse and fraud (for example, unauthorized sharing of one license). It is not used for profiling and is never sold or used for advertising.

2.3 Device ID and browser data

A randomly generated device ID, the browser's standard User-Agent, the extension version and activity timestamps are sent with license checks, only to enforce the number of devices per subscription and monitor performance.

2.4 Subscription / purchase status

The extension stores your subscription state (active/inactive/expired) and the payment method used (for example InstaPay or Vodafone Cash) to enable or block features.

2.5 Taxpayer ID and business data

Invoices and receipts you download are cached in your browser. When you use multi-device sync or the ERP features, the following are copied to our cloud databases (Supabase / Vercel), and invoice drafts to Google Firebase (Firestore), isolated per taxpayer ID: invoice and receipt metadata, customers, suppliers, items, drafts, treasuries, cash movements, shifts and settings. This is used only to power reports, ERP features, multi-device sharing and backup.

2.6 ERP connection credentials

If you use ERP submission, you may enter a Client ID and Client Secret. They are stored with the extension's settings on your device and may be copied to our cloud as part of settings sync. They are used only to request an access token from id.eta.gov.eg; the token itself stays in the extension's temporary memory.

2.7 Digital signing

When you sign invoices, the documents and the token PIN are sent only to the MYASigner application running on your own computer (localhost:5255). The PIN is not stored and the PIN, certificate and private key are never sent to any external server.

2.8 Error log, usage metrics and push

Truncated error messages raised by the extension's own code (message, page path, file and line) and database-usage counters are sent periodically. If you enable notifications, a push-subscription endpoint linked to your email is stored.

No data is used beyond the purposes above. The developer does not sell, rent or share your personal data with any third party, does not use it for ads, profiling or creditworthiness, and does not merge it with external data.

3 Data That Stays on Your Device

  • ETA API access tokens — kept only in the extension's temporary memory until they expire.
  • Signing PIN, certificate and private key — handled only by the MYASigner app on your computer.
  • Generated files (PDF/Excel and QR codes). QR codes are built locally with a bundled library and contain only the public invoice link.
  • Local cache in chrome.storage.local and the browser's IndexedDB, powering the statistics dashboard and offline use.
  • Device activity logs kept locally for troubleshooting.
Privacy guarantee: login passwords and your signing PIN are never read, stored or sent to the developer or any third party.

7 Data Security

  • Encryption in transit: all remote communication uses HTTPS/TLS only.
  • Isolation: each business's data is separated by taxpayer ID using row-level security (RLS) on the server.
  • Least privilege: ETA tokens and signing PINs are never stored on our servers.
  • Access control: only the developer can reach the databases, for subscription management, support and fraud prevention.
  • No secret keys in the extension: it uses public, restricted API keys; security is enforced server-side.
  • Local storage: local files and logs stay in your browser profile, protected by your OS and browser. No method is 100% secure; please protect your device and signing token.

8 Data Retention & Deletion

2 البيانات التي نجمعها ولماذا

2.1 البريد الإلكتروني

MYA PDF منتج برخصة مدفوعة. تقرأ الإضافة بريد المستخدم المسجل على بوابة الضرائب وترسله إلى خادم المطور (Supabase) للتحقق من وجود اشتراك نشط وفرض عدد الأجهزة.

2.2 عنوان IP

قد يُسجَّل عنوان IP من جهة الخادم مع طلبات فحص الترخيص، ويُستخدم حصرياً لمنع إساءة الاستخدام والاحتيال (مثل مشاركة ترخيص واحد بشكل غير مصرح). لا يُستخدم لبناء ملف تعريف عنك ولا يُباع ولا يُستخدم للإعلانات.

2.3 معرّف الجهاز وبيانات المتصفح

يُرسل معرّف جهاز عشوائي، وUser-Agent القياسي، ونسخة الإضافة وأوقات النشاط مع فحص الترخيص، لفرض عدد الأجهزة ومراقبة الأداء فقط.

2.4 حالة الاشتراك / الشراء

تُخزن الإضافة حالة اشتراكك (نشط/غير نشط/منتهي) وطريقة الدفع المستخدمة (مثل إنستاباي أو فودافون كاش) لتفعيل الميزات أو حظرها.

2.5 الرقم الضريبي وبيانات الأعمال

تُخزَّن الفواتير والإيصالات التي تنزّلها في متصفحك. وعند استخدام المزامنة بين الأجهزة أو ميزات ERP، تُنسخ البيانات التالية إلى قواعد بياناتنا السحابية (Supabase / Vercel)، ومسودات الفواتير إلى Google Firebase (Firestore)، مع عزل كل منشأة بالرقم الضريبي: بيانات الفواتير والإيصالات الوصفية، العملاء، الموردون، الأصناف، المسودات، الخزائن، حركات النقدية، الورديات والإعدادات. تُستخدم فقط لتشغيل التقارير وERP والمشاركة بين الأجهزة والنسخ الاحتياطي.

2.6 بيانات ربط ERP

عند استخدام الرفع عبر ERP قد تدخل Client ID وClient Secret. تُحفظ مع إعدادات الإضافة على جهازك وقد تُنسخ إلى سحابتنا ضمن مزامنة الإعدادات. تُستخدم فقط لطلب رمز دخول من id.eta.gov.eg، ويبقى الرمز نفسه في ذاكرة الإضافة المؤقتة.

2.7 التوقيع الإلكتروني
