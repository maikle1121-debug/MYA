Privacy Policy for MYA PDF

Last updated: August 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MYA PDF. This privacy policy explains how our Chrome Extension collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

2. Data Collection and Usage

MYA PDF is designed to assist you with eInvoices, Excel export, VAT returns, and invoice management on the Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) portal. The extension processes data locally on your device to generate PDFs and Excel files.

  • Local Processing: All data extracted from the ETA portal (such as invoice details) is processed locally on your computer.
  • No External Servers: We do not send, transmit, or store your personal data, invoice data, or sensitive information on any external servers.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties.

3. Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions to function correctly:

  • storage: Used to save your local settings and preferences.
  • tabs: Used to interact with the ETA portal tabs you have open.
  • downloads: Used to save generated PDF and Excel files directly to your device.
  • Host Permissions (invoicing.eta.gov.eg): Required to read and process invoice data directly from the official portal.

4. Data Security

Because the extension operates entirely locally, the security of your data depends on the security of your own device. We implement best practices in our code to ensure that your local data remains isolated and secure within the browser environment.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date of this Privacy Policy.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at the developer email provided in the Chrome Web Store.
