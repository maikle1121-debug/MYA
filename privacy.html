1 Short Summary

MYA PDF is a productivity tool that runs only on the official Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) e-invoicing portal (invoicing.eta.gov.eg). It lets you export your invoices to PDF/Excel and generate VAT reports. It is an independent individual developer product and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government entity.

Your financial data never leaves your computer. Your invoices, receipts, and reports are processed entirely on your own device and are stored only in your browser’s local storage. The extension does not transmit any invoice, receipt, or report content to any server.
Only licensing data is transmitted. To verify that a paid subscription is active and to enforce the number of permitted devices, the extension sends the email address used to log in to the ETA portal, a device identifier, a public IP address, and a technical user-agent string to the developer’s backend. This is the only data that ever leaves your device.

2 Data We Collect and Why

2.1 Email address

MYA PDF is a paid-license product. To activate the license, the extension reads the email address of the currently logged-in ETA portal user and sends it to the developer’s backend (Supabase). This is used solely to:

  • verify that a valid, paid subscription is associated with that email, and
  • confirm the subscription status before enabling export/report features.

2.2 Public IP address

The extension temporarily retrieves your public IP address via the public service api.ipify.org and forwards it to the developer’s backend together with the license check. This is used exclusively for abuse and fraud prevention — e.g., detecting unauthorized sharing or automated abuse of a single license. The IP is not used to profile you and is never sold or shared for advertising.

2.3 Device identifier & user agent

A locally generated device identifier (a random, non-personal token) together with the standard browser user-agent string is transmitted with the license check. Its only purpose is to enforce the number of devices that a single subscription may use. It cannot be used to identify you as an individual outside of your own subscription record.

2.4 Subscription / purchase status

The extension stores and transmits your subscription state (active/inactive/expired) and the payment method used to purchase the license (e.g., InstaPay / Vodafone Cash reference). This lets the extension correctly grant or block features.

No data is used for anything other than the purposes above. The developer does not sell, rent, trade, or share personal data with third parties for their own use, does not use it for advertising or profiling, and does not combine it with any third-party datasets.

3 Data That Never Leaves Your Device

The following data is processed exclusively on your own computer and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Invoice, receipt, and report content from your ETA portal session — used to build PDF/Excel exports and VAT reports.
  • Generated documents (PDF/Excel files, QR codes). QR codes are generated locally with a bundled library and contain only the invoice’s public link.
  • Document cache — invoice metadata the extension keeps in chrome.storage.local to render the statistics dashboard. This cache is stored in your browser profile and is not uploaded.
  • Device activity logs (last entries: license checks, errors) — stored in chrome.storage.local for troubleshooting and shown only to the developer’s dashboard when you explicitly connect it. You can clear them at any time from chrome://extensions → MYA PDF → Clear storage.
Privacy guarantee: nothing about the invoices you export, the reports you generate, or the files you download is ever visible to the developer or any third party.

7 Data Security

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the extension and the developer’s backend (Supabase) and every other service happens exclusively over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Minimal data principle: only the minimum licensing data is ever transmitted; invoice data is never sent.
  • Access control: only the developer can access the backend database; it is protected by strong authentication. Access is used strictly for subscription management and fraud prevention.
  • No secret keys shipped: the extension uses a publishable API key only, which is safe to be public; server-side rules enforce data access.
  • Local storage: all cached documents and logs stay in your browser profile, protected by your own operating system and browser.
